WiFi Share Card

Show a QR code for your WiFi so a phone can scan and connect in seconds. No typing the password. Just like the WiFi share feature on phones, now on your laptop.
🔒 100% local  •  Works offline  •  No sign up

1. Enter your WiFi details

Type the exact network name and password from your router, or use the auto-detect launcher for Windows below and it will fill these in for you.

Privacy note: The QR code contains your WiFi password so a phone can join automatically. Treat the card like the password itself. Do not post it publicly. This tool runs fully on your device and never sends your password anywhere.
What the QR code contains
WIFI: ...

This is the standard WiFi QR format supported by iPhone Camera and Android Camera / WiFi settings.

Fastest way on Windows: auto-detect
  1. Connect your laptop to the WiFi you want to share.
  2. Double-click Show-My-WiFi.ps1 (or the .bat file) that came with this tool.
  3. It reads the connected network name and password from Windows and opens this card already filled in.
  4. Scan the QR with your phone camera and tap Join / Connect.

Windows may ask you to allow the script. The password is read from the WiFi profile already saved on your own laptop, using the built-in netsh command.

How to connect on a phone
  1. iPhone: Open Camera, point at the QR, tap the WiFi notification, tap Join.
  2. Android: Open Camera or Settings, WiFi, QR icon, scan and tap Connect.
  3. If scanning does not work, type the name and password shown on the card.
